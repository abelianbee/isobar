## Tests run (chronological, everything tried is listed)
### H13 NBA spread overreaction to last game  -> DEAD
- N=27,030 team-games. Regress cover on prior game's cover margin: slope ~1e-4/pt, p=0.08. Direction right (teams covering by 15+ cover 47.4% next; -15 or worse cover 50.9%) but fading |prev cover margin|>10 wins 50.8% (N=11,562) vs 52.4% breakeven at -110. Not tradeable. No further work.

### H6 ATP overreaction to last result -> FAINT / DECAYED
- N=58k matches 2002-2026, closing odds (Pinnacle where available). Logit(win) ~ logit(p_close) + (own last-match surprise - opp's). Overall coef -0.012, p=0.59: nothing.
- Within-tournament subset (both players' previous match <=7 days ago, N=24k): the player whose previous-round win was MORE expected is underpriced (coef 0.21, p=0.002). Mechanism = market over-credits the underdog who just upset someone. But: strong in 2004-2014, gone in 2020-26 (coef 0.02, p=0.88). Betting sim at Pinnacle closing: ROI 0 to +1.5%, negative in 2020+. Market learned. Not deployable.
### H8 fatigue (prev-match minutes, sets, rest days) -> DEAD
- Joined TML match minutes to odds (86% match rate). N=18k within-tournament matches. Prev-match minutes diff: coef -0.029/hour, p=0.17 (right sign, tiny). 7-day cumulative minutes: p=0.67. Rest-days: p=0.9. Grand-slam-only: p=0.11. Bookmakers price fatigue adequately.
### H9 Elo vs market (ranking anchoring) -> INVERTED, weak
- Computed 538-style Elo + surface Elo from 67k matches. Elo alone: good (coef 0.78) but market dominates (corr 0.88 with market logit). Conditional on market price, Elo has NEGATIVE coefficient (-0.06 overall, -0.15 in 2020-26, p<0.001): when Elo likes a player more than the market, fade Elo. Market has private info (injury, form) that Elo lacks and the price under-states it.
- Trade: bet market favourite when market prob exceeds Elo prob by >0.15-0.2. Pinnacle-closing ROI: 2006-11 +1.2%, 2012-17 -3.4%, 2018-21 +1.8%, 2022-26 +3.5% (N~1000/era, se ~2.2%). OOS logistic (fit <=2017, bet EV>0 in 2018-26): +7.2% on N=587, se 4%. Inconsistent across eras; ~1.5-sigma. Parked, not deployable alone.
- Also confirmed favourite-longshot bias at Pinnacle: all favourites lose -0.5% (p>0.9) to -3.1% (p 0.5-0.6). Backing favourites is the cheaper side but not profitable at closing.
